#1Best Overall4.7★★★★★

Hakko FX-888DX

The FX-888D's replacement: the same T18 tips and handpiece, with a 100W station, a proper dial and five presets.

Best for:Serious hobbyists, repair benches

Hakko replaced the FX-888D with the FX-888DX. It keeps the FX-8801 handpiece and T18 tips, the 50–480°C range and ±1°C idle stability, but the station rises from 70W to 100W, swaps the old button interface for a push-button rotary dial with a digital °F/°C display, stores five presets and adds a parameter lock against accidental calibration. It is ESD safe and also drives Hakko's tweezers, solder gun and larger irons.

Why it fits: The Hakko FX-888DX is ideal for rework tasks due to its 100W station and precise temperature control. It maintains stable temperatures and is ESD safe, making it suitable for both through-hole and surface mount work. Its compatibility with other Hakko handpieces enhances its versatility, ensuring reliable performance for complex rework tasks.

Before you buy

Buying advice

Consider Power and Temperature Control

For rework tasks, choose a soldering iron with high power and precise temperature control. The Hakko FX-888DX, for example, has a 100W station and digital closed-loop temperature control, ensuring stable temperatures. This precision is crucial for both through-hole and surface mount work, preventing damage to sensitive components during rework. Additionally, a quick heat-up time is essential for efficient work, especially when switching between different tasks.

Versatility for Different Tasks

Choose a soldering iron that can handle both through-hole and surface mount work. The YIHUA 862BD+ offers a dual-purpose setup with a hot air rework station, providing a wide temperature range and precise control for both soldering and desoldering tasks. This versatility makes it ideal for comprehensive rework tasks, ensuring you have the right tool for every job.

Safety and Compatibility

Ensure your soldering iron is ESD safe and compatible with various handpieces. The Hakko FX-888DX, for instance, is ESD safe and works with other Hakko handpieces, enhancing its utility for professional use. This compatibility ensures you can use the iron with a variety of tools, making it a versatile choice for any rework task. Additionally, a robust build quality and reliable performance are essential for consistent results.
